01 · What we found

What we saw when we looked at YAZIO on social.

Before writing anything, we looked at how people talk about YAZIO and calorie-tracking apps today. Four things stood out.

60M+ downloads

A top calorie tracker, but the creator layer is thin.

YAZIO has over 60 million downloads and more than 1 million paying subscribers. It ranks in the top 15 in Health & Fitness in several European markets. Yet most of what shows up under #yazio on TikTok is organic user content, not briefed creators speaking to local audiences.

People search for it

TikTok is a search engine for calorie counting.

Searches like "app para contar calorias", "calorie counter app" and "melhor app de dieta" already have active TikTok discover pages. Videos made for those searches keep bringing installs long after they are posted. It's the approach we designed for Flo's US rollout.

The AI angle

One photo, every calorie. That is a hook.

YAZIO rebranded as an AI Calorie Tracker in late 2025, with a meal scanner that counts calories from a single photo. That five-second moment on camera is the kind of hook that stops a scroll and makes someone install.

Three untapped markets

Spain, Brazil and Mexico are wide open.

YAZIO already supports Spanish and Portuguese, with local food databases in each market. Nutrition content is booming on TikTok in all three countries, but YAZIO has almost no creator presence there. The cost per creator is a fraction of the US or UK.

Sources: YAZIO press page (60M+ downloads); Business of Apps on YAZIO user base; TikTok discover pages for "app para contar calorias" and "calorie counter app". Checked November 2026.

04 · A wildcard worth testing

Four creator types nobody expects from a calorie tracker.

Next to the shortlist, we'd keep a small part of the first wave for four creator types outside the usual nutrition crowd. A few creators each, measured like the rest. If one works, it becomes a channel of its own.

Wildcard 1

Cooking ASMR creators

Millions watch food being chopped, sizzled and plated in silence. A calorie counter popping up on screen after each ingredient fits the visual rhythm without breaking the mood. The scan becomes part of the ASMR.

"Watch me make this. Then watch the calories add up."
#cookingazmr · #asmrfood · #satisfying
Wildcard 2

"What I eat in a day" creators

The biggest nutrition format on TikTok, filmed by thousands of creators every day. Most of them guess their calories. The ones who scan each meal with YAZIO stand out and give their audience something to act on.

"What I eat in a day, but this time I actually counted."
#whatieatinaday · #wieiad · #fulldayofeating
Wildcard 3

Students on a budget

University students cooking cheap, healthy meals in tiny kitchens. A calorie tracker that also shows how affordable a balanced day can be. Price per meal plus calories per meal, in one video.

"Three meals, under 10 euros, under 1,800 calories."
#studentmeals · #uni · #budgetfood
Wildcard 4

Meal prep for couples and families

Couples and parents who plan and cook a full week together. Scanning each container as it gets packed, with a running total on screen. Practical content that helps two people eat better, not just one.

"Sunday meal prep for two. 14 meals, every calorie tracked."
#mealprep · #couplegoals · #familymeals
How we'd test it: two or three creators per wildcard inside the first wave, each with their own link, so you can compare cost per install against the main shortlist. We'd scout these creators after our call.
05 · A first wave
