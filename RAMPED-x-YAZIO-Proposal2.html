01 · The Opportunity

You already won Europe. The next chapter is creator-led.

Nutrition is the most repeated decision people make — three-plus times a day, every day. And the way that decision spreads on social isn't an ad; it's a creator showing what she actually eats, how she lost the weight, how she finally stuck to a habit. YAZIO is one of the most downloaded nutrition apps in Europe, with 100M+ users, a million paying subscribers and a product that's deeply localised — a food database built for real local groceries, not a US-centric one. What's missing in Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking markets isn't product fit. It's the engine that turns "I saw it on my feed" into trackable installs and YAZIO Pro subscribers, run natively by local creators.

Product-market fit is already there

YAZIO is localised for these markets — Spanish and Portuguese, with local foods and brands in the database. That's the hard part, and it's done. The gap is distribution: putting the app in front of the audience through people they already trust.

The cheapest reach is where you're least invested

Spanish- and Portuguese-language creator markets deliver some of the lowest costs per view anywhere — while the US and UK, the crowded English battleground, cost multiples more. This is where a euro of budget buys the most attention.

02 · The Strategy

Don't bet on one creator size. Layer them.

The most common mistake is spending a whole budget on one big name — or only on cheap nano creators. Each tier does a different job. Combined, they cover the full funnel: reach and credibility at the top, conversion in the middle, authenticity and volume at the base. We build this same pyramid in each market, casting locally across nutrition, fitness, recipe, weight-loss and lifestyle niches.

1

Cast across tiers

Ramped sources a mix of nano, micro and mid creators in nutrition, fitness, recipes, fasting and weight-loss journeys — native to each market, briefed for compliance.

2

Brief & create

Each creator lives a real YAZIO habit — tracking meals, scanning food, running a 16:8 fast — and produces authentic content, with paid usage rights included.

3

Concentrated launch

Creators post in the same window. The install spike lifts App Store ranking in Health & Fitness — extra organic installs on top.

4

Amplify winners

Top organic videos are boosted as paid ads. You spend media budget only on creative already proven to convert.

Why combine sizes (and not just go big)

A single big creator gives you one shot, one audience, one CPM. Ten layered creators give you ten audiences, ten content angles to test, and a portfolio that de-risks the campaign. If one underperforms, the budget moves — you're never all-in on a single bet.

Why layering drives more reach

Mid-tier nutrition and fitness voices create the awareness wave and credibility; micro creators deliver the best engagement and lowest cost per install; nano creators add authentic volume and tight-knit recipe and weight-loss communities. People who see the same app from several creators they trust convert far better than from one big post.

05 · The Original Idea

Three markets aren't three campaigns. They're one engine.

Here's the move most agencies miss. Spain and Mexico share a language, and all three markets share the same buyer — someone who wants to eat better and finally stick to it. So the winning hooks, the strongest creator formats and the Spark-ready UGC don't stay in one market. We treat the three as a single learning system: each market plays a role, and the creative learning compounds across all of them.

🇪🇸 Test & prove in Spain

Ramped's home turf and a low-cost market to prove the system fast. Spain generates the first winning hooks and a Spanish-language content library that ports straight to Mexico.

🇧🇷 Scale in Brazil

The largest, most creator-native market of the three — and one where we've already run this exact model for a subscription health app. Huge, low-cost Portuguese reach.

🇲🇽 Expand in Mexico

The Spanish creative proven in Spain lands here at a fraction of the effort — same language, same angles — opening the wider Spanish-speaking LATAM audience.

The result: learning compounds across borders. This is what's possible as you add markets — not a requirement. Start with one; if you add a second or third later, Spain de-risks Mexico, Brazil brings proven scale, and one performance-tested UGC library serves them all. You learn faster, and you only put paid budget behind creative that's already proven to convert.

07 · Guarantee & Terms

The risk sits with us, not with you.

This is what makes the model safe to try in any market: you don't pre-fund anything, you only pay for work that actually goes live, and every campaign carries a guaranteed view commitment.

Guaranteed views

  • We agree a minimum number of views per campaign up front — so performance is committed, not hoped for.
  • If a creator's content doesn't hit the agreed views, we reshoot or repost until it does.
  • If it still falls short, you don't pay for that creator. The view floor protects your spend.
  • Guaranteed views give you a known reach baseline to plan installs and subscribers around.

How you pay — no upfront risk

  • Invoiced at the end of each month — never before.
